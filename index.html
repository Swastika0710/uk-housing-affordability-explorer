Methods and limitations

The affordability ratio is calculated in the application as average house price divided by average annual earnings:

affordability ratio = house price / annual earnings

The “years of full earnings to buy” figure divides the house price by annual earnings. The deposit figure shows 10% of the selected region’s average house price.

Important limitation: This demonstration currently uses illustrative regional estimates, not official ONS or HM Land Registry statistics. The figures use average values rather than the median-based measures used in many official affordability statistics, so they are not directly comparable with official publications. A future version will replace these estimates with sourced, openly licensed data and record the download date.

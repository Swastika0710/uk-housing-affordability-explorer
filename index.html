Research note

The affordability ratio compares average house prices with average annual earnings. A higher ratio means housing is less affordable relative to local earnings.

Data note: This demonstration uses illustrative regional estimates for research and portfolio purposes. It is not an official statistical release and should not be used for policy decisions.
